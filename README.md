CSEC2001 Secure Scripting and Business Applications
PostgreSQL lab environment (De Montfort University Cambodia)
This repository gives you a ready-made Ubuntu machine in your browser with PostgreSQL installed.
You do not need administrator rights on the lab computer, and nothing is installed on it.
Open your lab machine
Sign in to GitHub with your own personal account (create one at github.com if you do not have one).
On this page, click the green Code button, choose the Codespaces tab, then Create codespace on main.
Wait two to three minutes. A VS Code window opens in your browser with a terminal at the bottom.
When you see `PostgreSQL is installed and running`, you are ready.
Follow Lab 1 from Step 5 onward.
Coming back later
Your codespace keeps your work. Open github.com/codespaces to reopen it.
When you finish a lab session
Go to github.com/codespaces and choose Stop (or Delete once you submit the lab).
A free account includes 60 hours a month on this machine size, and a stopped codespace still uses
your storage allowance, so delete codespaces you no longer need.
